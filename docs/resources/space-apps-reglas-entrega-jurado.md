2026 theme: **"The Next Frontier"**. Event: **14–15 November 2026**.

## 1. What the submission must contain (Project Submission)

### Submission window

- **Opens:** Saturday 14 November, **9:00 a.m.** local time of your Local Event.
- **Closes:** Sunday 15 November, **11:59 p.m.** local time of your Local Event.
- **Universal Event:** same hours, in the local time of the **Team Owner's** residence.
- The team page's **"Project"** tab appears at 9:00 a.m. on 14 November (local time).
- You can edit and resubmit as many times as you want until the deadline. **Judges only see the
  last saved submission.** Anything saved or sent after the deadline is not judged.
- You must click **"Submit for Judging"** before the deadline. Saving is not enough.

### Form fields

| Field | What to put |
|---|---|
| **Project Name** | The project's name. |
| **High-Level Project Summary** | What you developed, how it answers the challenge and why it matters. |
| **Link to Project Demo** | Public link to a **video of 30 seconds or less** or a **deck of at most 7 slides, counting title and intro**. |
| **Link to Final Project** | Public link to the final project: website, repo (GitHub), Drive, YouTube, etc. |
| **Detailed Project Description** | What it does, how it works, benefits, objectives, and tools, languages, hardware and software used. |
| **Use of Super Intelligence (SI)** | Whether you used AI tools (the site calls them "SI") and how. See section 4. |
| **NASA Data** | Which NASA data and resources you used. **Global Award eligibility requires at least one NASA datum or resource.** |
| **Space Agency Partner Data and Other References** | **All** data, resources and tools used, including code, text and images, even open source or free ones. Copyrighted material requires permission. |

### Limits and format

- **Mandatory demo:** video of **30 seconds or less** or slides of **at most 7, counting title and
  intro**. **Projects exceeding these limits are not eligible for Global Judging.**
- **Language:** everything in **English**: the submission page and all linked material. Only
  exception: the demo video may be spoken in another language if it has **English subtitles**.
  Slides must be in English.
- **Files:** the project page accepts **JPG, PNG or GIF images up to 10 MB**. Videos and documents
  cannot be uploaded; host them externally (YouTube, Google Drive, GitHub, OneDrive, Dropbox) and
  paste the link.
- **Public links:** no login, permissions, password or registration. If judges cannot open the
  project, they cannot evaluate it. Test every link in a private window before submitting.
- **Own website (optional, recommended):** request a rating at the
  [FortiGuard Web Filter](https://www.fortiguard.com/faq/wfratingsubmit) so network filters do not
  block the site for judges. "Company Name" = "N/A".
- **Minors:** demos and projects **cannot include images, video, audio or any likeness of people
  under 18**.

### Checkboxes when submitting

1. Accept the **Terms and Conditions**.
2. **"Confirm All Work is Original"**: confirm the work is original and all external resources are
   listed in References.
3. Click **"Submit for Judging"**. A confirmation banner appears on success.

### Team requirements for judging

- All members must be **confirmed in the same Local Event** (not waitlist) or in the Universal
  Event.
- All mandatory fields complete and in English.

### Suggested 30-second video structure

- `0:00` Introduce yourself and the team.
- `0:02` Which challenge you address and why.
- `0:05` Project name and tagline.
- `0:10` How it works: images or prototype, user experience, and how NASA and partner data make it
  possible.
- `0:20–0:30` Where it can go and what it can achieve.

Slides must include team name, member names, chosen challenge, a project image, the user
experience and how NASA data is used.

Source: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/

## 2. Judging criteria and awards (Judging & Awards)

### Global Judging eligibility (minimums)

- Answer one of the official challenges.
- All members registered in the **same** Local Event (not waitlist) or Universal Event, visible in
  the Members tab. **Members cannot be added after the deadline.**
- Team size: **minimum 1 in a Local Event, minimum 2 in the Universal Event, maximum 6.**
- All mandatory fields complete, demo within limits, everything in English.
- Accept both checkboxes (terms and originality, with all sources in References).

### The 5 criteria (each scored 1–5 at every stage)

| Criterion | What it evaluates |
|---|---|
| **Impact** | Size of the problem and reach of the effect: how many people it inspires or helps. |
| **Creativity** | Originality and innovation: a new concept or a significant improvement over existing ones. |
| **Validity** | Scientific basis and technical, functional or logical feasibility; completeness, usability and ease of use. |
| **Relevance** | How fully it answers the challenge and how much it **integrates NASA data as a central component**, not a superficial add-on. |
| **Presentation** | Communication and storytelling: whether the challenge, solution and importance are clear. **Evaluated with the demo and the full project.** |

### Stages

1. **Phase 1: Local Event Judging & Global Nominees** — announced **3 December 2026**.
2. **Phase 2: Global Finalists & Honorable Mentions** — announced **17 December 2026**.
3. **Phase 3: Global Winners** — announced **26 January 2027**.

### The 10 Global Awards

Best Use of Science · Best Use of Data · Best Use of Technology · Galactic Impact · Best Mission
Concept · Most Inspirational · Best Storytelling Award · Global Connection Award · Art &
Technology Award · Local Impact Award.

Source: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide/

## 3. Key rules (Participant FAQ and Terms & Conditions)

- **Free.** If anyone asks for money, do not pay and report to info@spaceappschallenge.org.
- **Open to all ages.** Under-18s must be registered by a parent or guardian who accompanies them
  at all times; they cannot use their photo as avatar or their image, voice or likeness (or other
  minors') in the project.
- **Teams:** max 6; min 1 in a Local Event, min 2 in the Universal Event. One team per person, one
  challenge per team.
- **No work on challenges before the hackathon.** Challenge resources were published 13 November.
- **IP and licenses:** by submitting you grant NASA permission to show, copy, combine, distribute
  and disseminate your solution. Original content must be freely available or under an
  **OSI-approved open source license**. **Without a stated license, NASA distributes under
  Apache 2.0.** Keep an explicit `LICENSE` (MIT or Apache-2.0). Third-party material must also be
  public or OSI-licensed, with the license documented. **Cite every source, including open
  source.** No ITAR/EAR-controlled information, no PII, no commercial promotion, no content you
  lack rights to.
- **NASA brand:** generated content **cannot use, contain or modify any NASA brand or design
  element** (logos, flags, mission identifiers). Safe practice for the whole project: no NASA
  logo in UI, slides, favicon or video, and no suggestion of NASA endorsement. Naming "NASA data"
  in text to cite sources is fine.

Sources: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/participant-faqs/ ·
https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/legal/

## 4. Use of AI ("Super Intelligence", SI)

Per the FAQ, the site uses **"Super Intelligence (SI)"** instead of "Artificial Intelligence (AI)"
(US Executive Order of 29 September 2026). The submission form uses that term.

- **SI tools are allowed** (LLMs, chatbots, image/video generation, foundation models, code
  generation) to accelerate development and enhance creativity.
- **Not allowed:** using SI **to conceive the idea** of how to solve the challenge.
- The submission **must describe how SI was used** ("Use of Super Intelligence (SI)" field).
- **Generated content must be marked:** images and videos with a **visible watermark**; audio
  with a note in the descriptive text and metadata; code and data with a note in the descriptive
  text and metadata (in practice: a README section, file comments or an `AI_USAGE.md`).
- Generated content cannot use or modify NASA brand elements.
- The SI field response is not seen by judges (data collection only), but judges **do see**
  watermarks, texts and metadata inside the project, and evaluate the **originality, intent and
  execution** of the SI-assisted parts.

Sources: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/ ·
https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/participant-faqs/

## Quick checklist before submitting

- [ ] Everything in English. If the video is spoken in another language, English subtitles.
- [ ] Demo ≤30 seconds or ≤7 slides counting title and intro.
- [ ] Demo and final project links tested in a private window, no login required.
- [ ] At least one NASA datum or resource genuinely used and named in "NASA Data".
- [ ] References list everything: datasets, libraries, images, fonts, APIs.
- [ ] Repo has an explicit OSI `LICENSE`.
- [ ] SI use declared; generated images/videos watermarked; generated code/data marked in README
      or metadata.
- [ ] No NASA logos; no images, voices or likenesses of minors.
- [ ] All members in the same Local Event (not waitlist), ≤6 members.
- [ ] "Submit for Judging" clicked before 11:59 p.m. local time, confirmation banner visible.
